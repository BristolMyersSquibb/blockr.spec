Plans for changes to blockr too big for one spec. Each rewrite is a tree of pages: a root page, parts that each become a spec once settled, and design pages that set out an API in full. A page's address is its ID, which never changes, and its name is the heading it opens with, which may. Its second line names its state: draft, review, settled, implementing, closed, done or dropped. A page lists the pages under it in its body, and its sidebar leads to the top, up to its parent and down to its children, each with its state. The [roadmap](Roadmap) says when each part is specced and implemented, and what comes after the rewrites, and [Later additions](Later-additions) lists smaller additions that are not ready for a spec.

The pages live in [BristolMyersSquibb/blockr.spec](https://github.com/BristolMyersSquibb/blockr.spec): a change reaches this wiki when its pull request there merges, and an edit made here is carried over to that repo.
