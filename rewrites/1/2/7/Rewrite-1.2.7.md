# Block cards in the dock
Status: draft. Design for [Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2).

One of the design pages of the client-drawn dock ([Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2)): the card that the dock draws around each block.
It covers the card's header, after [blockr#492](https://github.com/orgs/BristolMyersSquibb/discussions/492), with the block's mark, name, status and toggles, its sections, which show as core's attribute `visible` says, its conditions, its menu, compact headers, and the card on a locked board.
It builds on [Drawing the board](Rewrite-1.1.8#what-core-gives-plugins), through which core mounts the block's UI and output in the card.
What extensions add to a card is [The dock's contribution points](Rewrite-1.2.4)'s.

## What must hold

Condition 4 of [Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2#what-must-hold) rests on this page for block cards.
