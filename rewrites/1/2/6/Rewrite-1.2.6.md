# The dock's services
Status: draft. Design for [Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2).

One of the design pages of the client-drawn dock ([Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2)): what the dock offers extensions besides places to draw in.
It covers notifications, and storage, which saves and opens boards, with a download and an upload by default, and which session management replaces ([Session management as a client extension](Rewrite-1.6)).
It also covers what the dock tells extensions about the screen, such as which panel has focus.
It builds on [Registering extensions with the dock](Rewrite-1.2.3).

## What must hold

Condition 3 of [Client-drawn dock and extensions](Rewrite-1.2#what-must-hold) rests on this page, with [Registering extensions with the dock](Rewrite-1.2.3), [The dock's contribution points](Rewrite-1.2.4) and [The dock's navbar](Rewrite-1.2.5).
